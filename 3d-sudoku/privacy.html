3D SUDOKU · PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how 3D Sudoku (the "App"), made by Capital Tech, handles information on iOS and Android.

What we collect

Nothing. The App has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It does not use the internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your current puzzle, notes and settings are saved only on your device so you can continue later. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you uninstall the App.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data, because we do not collect any.

Retention and deletion

There is no data on our side to delete. Uninstalling the App deletes everything it stored on your device.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
