Privacy Policy · The Ten Promised Paradise

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains how العشرة المبشرون بالجنة – The Ten Promised Paradise (the "App"), made by Capital Tech, handles information on iOS and Android.

What we collect

Nothing. The App has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It does not use the internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (stars and stories heard), the name you type for the certificate and your language choice are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you uninstall the App.

Speech and sharing

Text is read aloud by your device's own text-to-speech engine. When you share the certificate, the image is created on your device and sent only to the app you choose.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data, because we do not collect any.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
