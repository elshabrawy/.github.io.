Moto Rush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Moto Rush (the "App"), developed by Shabrawy Apps ("we", "us"), handles information when you play the game on Android.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, address, or phone number on our own servers.

Google Play Games Services

The App uses Google Play Games Services for sign-in and the High Score leaderboard. When you sign in, Google provides your Play Games player ID, display name, and avatar, and your scores are submitted to Google's leaderboard. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to show personalized ads where permitted. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings (for example your best score and sound settings) are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

To delete your Play Games data, use your Google account's Play Games settings. You can also contact us for help.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
